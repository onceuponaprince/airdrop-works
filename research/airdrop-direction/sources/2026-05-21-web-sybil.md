## Market size and pain

- Sybil attacks remain a top-three cause of misallocated airdrop value; industry analyses cite billions in lost or dumped token value and governance capture (ChainAware 2026 roundup).
- Aptos airdrop cited in industry literature: large fraction of allocation claimed by Sybil-style addresses.
- DAO participation averages remain low (~17% in cited governance studies), amplifying attacker leverage.

## Incumbent categories

### Proof-of-human / credential aggregators
- **Human Passport** (formerly Gitcoin Passport): stamp aggregation, humanity score, integrated with Guild/Galxe/Snapshot; Sybil Protection Suite positioning.
- **Humanity Protocol**: biometric-backed PoH, Fairdrops product for Sybil-proof drops.
- **Proof of Humanity**: primary-source verification vs aggregator model.

### Wallet graph / behavioural Sybil APIs
- **Cred Protocol**: Sybil detection API, composite score 0-100, ~$0.03 USDC per request (x402), API token pricing in Cred units.
- **Trusta Labs / TrustScan**: Sybil Score API, enterprise quotas (trial 1k calls; negotiable QPS/monthly caps).
- **ChainAware.ai**: behavioural + governance framing.
- **WalletTag Score**: real-time EVM wallet trust API.
- **Nomis, RubyScore, ReputeX**: reputation filters.

## Pricing signal

- Wallet-level checks trending toward **commodity API pricing** (cents per lookup).
- Differentiation shifting to **campaign tooling** and **behavioural graphs**, not single-score novelty.

## Whitespace (quality vs humanity)

- Zama Creator Program (2025): **monthly quality scoring** with public parameters, engagement caps, anti-bot safeguards — closer to **contribution judge** than stamp collection.
- Industry narrative moving from "one human = one wallet" to "one human still farms with low-effort content" (Human Tech blog, 2026).

## Implications for spin-off A

- Selling "another Passport" is a losing GTM lane.
- Selling **second-layer content quality + farming flag after identity** matches protocol pain post-Passport adoption.
- Account-level `farmingPercentage` + per-post rubric is differentiated vs pure on-chain graph scores.
