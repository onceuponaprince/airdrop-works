## Market pain (B2B SaaS / performance)

- Last-touch attribution widely regarded as broken for B2B SaaS (6-10 stakeholders, 84-180 day cycles, dark funnel).
- Estimates of **30-60% misallocated marketing spend** under weak attribution (ORM, Pepper Effect 2026 playbooks).
- **Marketing mix modeling (MMM)** resurgence in 2026: channel interaction effects, diminishing returns curves, post-cookie viability.

## Implications for spin-off B

- **Crowded** at "score my ad before launch" — incumbents have data flywheels and enterprise sales.
- **Whitespace for a solo builder** only at: (1) niche vertical rubrics, (2) crypto-native marketing teams, (3) **combined copy + social proof judge** if differentiated from Meta connector incumbents.
- Without ad-platform integrations, product is **copilot** not **system of record** — harder to charge $2k+/mo.
- Faster PLG loop than protocol enterprise, but **brand mismatch** for `airdrop.works` domain.
