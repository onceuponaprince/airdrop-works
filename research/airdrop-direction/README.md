# AI(r)Drop strategic direction — living research

**Status: Episode 1 complete — synthesis in `episodes/01-spinoff-vs-core.md`, pilot at `pilot.html`.**

## What this is

Prince is deciding where to take **airdrop-works** after building an AI Judge that scores Web3 contributions (quality dimensions + farming/abuse flags). Two spin-off ideas are on the table: **(A)** a reputation layer that stops scalpers, bots, and farmers; **(B)** a marketing tool that uses AI to judge creatives and maximise spend. This thread tests which direction earns the next twelve months of effort — and whether both can coexist as wedges on one engine.

The claim under test is stated once and tested across episodes:

> **Contributor reputation (content-quality + farming detection) should be the primary product bet for airdrop-works; a marketing-judge SKU is a fast experiment, not a full pivot — unless PLG proves otherwise within 30 days.**

If the claim holds, the team sharpens `airdrop.works` for protocols and creator programs, strips gamification to demo chrome, and runs a thin B2B marketing rubric as a separate surface. If the claim falls, a rebranded MarTech fork becomes the lead vehicle and Web3 becomes optional vertical.
