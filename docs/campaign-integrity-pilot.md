# Campaign Integrity Pilot — one-pager

**AI(r)Drop** scores Web3 contributions by quality and farming risk — not wallet age alone.

**Site:** [airdrop.works](https://airdrop.works) · **Product phase:** Phase 1 (platform + pilot offer)

---

## The problem

Most distributions still optimise for **volume**: likes, retweets, quest completions, wallet count. Farmers and bots pass basic Sybil checks, then capture allocation with low-effort content. Communities see “farmers won the drop”; teams lose trust and face sell pressure.

**Proof-of-humanity tools** (Human Passport, Humanity Protocol, wallet graph APIs) answer: *Is this likely one human?*

They do **not** answer: *Did this account add real teaching, originality, or community value — or farm engagement?*

---

## What we do (second layer)

AI(r)Drop’s **AI Judge** evaluates **content** and rolls up **accounts**:

| Output | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Dimension scores | Teaching value, originality, community impact (0–100) |
| Farming flag | `genuine` · `farming` · `ambiguous` per post |
| Explanation | Short rationale for allocators and appeals |
| Account rollup | Farming % across a sample of recent posts |
| Export | CSV of ranked contributors and flags (pilot deliverable) |

Works **after** Passport or similar — we do not replace identity; we reduce misallocation on humans who still farm.

## Why not only LLM or only Sybil API?

| Approach | Limit |
|----------|--------|
| ChatGPT paste | No scale, no audit trail, inconsistent rubric |
| Wallet Sybil score | No text quality; farmers with aged wallets pass |
| Quest platform defaults | Volume-first; limited explainability |

AI(r)Drop combines **rubric + farming flag + cache + account batch** in one pipeline you can defend to the community.
