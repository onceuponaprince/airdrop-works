# Content Builder brief — AI(r)Drop / airdrop.works

**Profile:** `airdrop-marketing` (Content Builder / content-engine-service) — not `general` or adult-lane profiles.

## Voice (explicit direction — confirm and apply)
- Register: sharp Web3-native, contributor-first, anti-hype. No lambo language, no "guaranteed airdrop" promises.
- Person: "we" for product (Yurika / AI(r)Drop team); "you" for reader (builder, creator, community lead).
- Tone: credible operator building in public — proof over puff. Short sentences on social; fuller argument on blog.
- Signature moves: name the broken incentive (farmers win, real contributors lose); one coined frame per week max (e.g. "quality-weighted airdrop", "score before spray"); concrete CTAs only (demo, waitlist, donate) — never vague "learn more".
- Banned: "100x", "moon", "WAGMI", fake urgency, implying financial advice or guaranteed token gains.

## Product context
- **Product:** AI(r)Drop at https://airdrop.works — AI scores Web3 contributions (Twitter, on-chain activity) so airdrops reward real builders, not bots.
- **North-star (4 weeks):** (1) waitlist completions, (2) donations on Base + Solana, (3) paid signup after whitelist approval.
- **Funnel:** Landing demo (no login) → waitlist (wallet + email, ~2 min) → Twitter account analyzer → pricing/waitlist → post-login dashboard activation.
- **Chains:** Base, Solana, Avalanche (rewards narrative); donations Base + Solana mainnet.
- **Audience:** Crypto-native founders, community leads, creators tired of farmer-dominated drops; secondary: donors who want to fund fair infra.
- **Handles:** @yuaboratory (build account); product site airdrop.works.

## Deliverable
Produce a **4-week content calendar** (starting week of 2026-05-26) as a single markdown doc titled `content-calendar-airdrop-works.md`.

### Required structure
1. **Executive summary** — goals, primary KPIs, channel mix rationale (≤200 words).
2. **Weekly themes table** — 4 rows: week label, theme, north-star tie-in, hero asset to produce.
3. **Day-by-day calendar** — for each week, Mon–Sun rows with columns:
   - Date (ISO)
   - Channel (Twitter/X, blog, Discord announcement, optional LinkedIn)
   - Post type (thread, single, blog teaser, demo clip concept, waitlist push, donate thank-you, BTS build)
   - Hook (≤15 words)
   - Body outline (3–5 bullets)
   - Primary CTA (demo / waitlist / donate / retweet — one only)
   - UTM slug suggestion
4. **Campaign arcs** — map posts to: (A) trust/demo, (B) waitlist social proof, (C) Twitter analyzer wow, (D) donate/support build, (E) post-login activation.
5. **Reuse matrix** — which blog pieces become threads; which demo moments become clips.
6. **Measurement** — GA events to watch per week (`marketing_demo_complete`, `waitlist_submit_success`, `twitter_analyze_complete`, `donate_success`, `pricing_plan_click`).
7. **Risks & guardrails** — what not to post during waitlist-gated beta; compliance notes (no token price talk).

### Cadence targets
- Twitter/X: 5–7 posts/week (mix singles + 1 thread).
- Blog: 1 piece/week (800–1200 words) or biweekly deep-dive alternating with short lesson.
- 1 "build in public" BTS post/week tied to shipped marketing fixes.
- Weekend: lighter recap or community poll.

### Content pillars (rotate)
1. Problem — broken airdrops / farmer incentive
2. Proof — demo score, analyzer screenshot, waitlist counter
3. Product — how AI judge works (no jargon dump)
4. Community — waitlist rank, referral loop
5. Support — donate / infra costs (transparent, separate from waitlist)

Use content type **`content-calendar`**. Stage the full calendar markdown to the workspace output directory.
